# Durable restrictions and quirks

(authored by agents unless marked 🧑)

## Pricing, account, and payment restrictions

- **restriction:** Durable's [pricing page](https://durable.com/pricing) lists
  Free at `$0`, Launch at `$25/month` (`$22/month` in its annual comparison),
  and Grow at `$49/month` (`$41/month` annually). The cheapest paid route is
  above a `$12` total budget.
- **restriction:** the [terms](https://durable.com/terms-of-service) say paid
  fees are advance, recurring, normally non-cancelable, and non-refundable;
  tax is extra. A billing-backed trial automatically charges when it ends unless
  canceled.
- **account fact:** the Free signup embedded on the [pricing page](https://durable.com/pricing)
  says no credit card is required.

## Free tier, sites, pages, and publishing restrictions

- **restriction—provider conflict:** the pricing page says Free can “Publish to
  .durable.site,” but the [builder FAQ](https://durable.com/ai-website-builder)
  says a paid subscription is required to publish. Public Free publishing is
  therefore not dependable until Durable resolves the conflict.
- **restriction:** the pricing comparison says AI-generated pages are unlimited
  on all plans. This is a page allowance, not a distinct-site allowance.
- **restriction:** Durable documents adding another business and website in
  [one account](https://help.durable.com/en/articles/15199789-create-a-new-website-by-adding-a-business).

## Content-generation restrictions

- **restriction:** [pricing](https://durable.com/pricing) limits the Free Blog
  Writer to two posts; Launch gets ten posts/month and Grow gets 32 posts/month.
  Free also has five generated images and ten AI chat messages per month.

## Branding, domain, and persistence restrictions

- **restriction:** Free uses a `*.durable.site` subdomain, cannot connect an
  external domain, and displays a “Built with Durable” badge. The
  [badge guide](https://help.durable.com/en/articles/15199759-removing-the-built-with-durable-branding-from-your-website)
  requires a paid plan to remove it.
- **restriction:** the [terms](https://durable.com/terms-of-service) require
  written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the subscription term
  ends; paid fees are non-cancelable and non-refundable. The terms do not
  promise that a hosted site or its URL persists after termination.
- **restriction:** regenerating a whole site permanently deletes the prior
  version and cannot be undone, according to the
  [regeneration guide](https://help.durable.com/en/articles/15199859-how-to-regenerate-your-entire-website).

## Export, crawl, robots, and login restrictions

- **crawl fact:** published sites have an automatically generated sitemap at
  `/sitemap`, according to the
  [sitemap guide](https://help.durable.com/en/articles/15199802-view-your-website-sitemap).

## Automation and bulk restrictions

- **restriction:** the [terms](https://durable.com/terms-of-service) prohibit
  access for benchmarking or competitive purposes and prohibit scraping
  information about Durable, its service, or customers. They also limit use to
  the current plan's rates and the customer's internal business purposes.

- **verified unknown:** Free publishing remains unresolved because the pricing

- **verified unknown:** the terms do not promise that a hosted site or URL
