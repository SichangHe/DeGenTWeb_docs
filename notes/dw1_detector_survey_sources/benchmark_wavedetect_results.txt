Scope and limitations
The official public checkpoint and loader were used, but the harness calls the
underlying batched model directly because the official predict helper accepts one
string at a time. The local discrimination sample is a reproducible screen, not an
evaluation protocol or threshold calibration. It is not stratified by domain,
generator, date, or document source; stored comparator scores may have been
generated under different software versions and truncation limits; and some
historical source files are absent. No claim is made about calibrated accuracy,
robustness, sustained throughput, multi-worker contention, or production fit.
