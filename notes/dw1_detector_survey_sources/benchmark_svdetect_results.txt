Scope and limitations
This is an optimized batched reconstruction of the released feature mathematics,
not the exact release path, an end-to-end detector timing, or an accuracy
reproduction. The authors released training and evaluation code but no trained
steering directions or logistic-regression state. Deterministic unit directions
were used only to preserve tensor shapes; their scores have no classification
meaning. The result shows that an optimized reconstruction fits the hardware. It
does not establish deployed throughput, paper accuracy, or a production threshold.
