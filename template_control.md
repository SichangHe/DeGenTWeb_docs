# Body-swap OOD framing

Body swapping applies to existing negative samples: their bodies are replaced
with LLM-generated text. The resulting constructed pages are synthetic
out-of-distribution (OOD) samples. Results on them must be reported as an OOD
stress test, not as in-distribution accuracy or evidence about Wix or B12.

The earlier Wix/B12 body-swap prototype resulted from a mistaken framing. Its
run and summary are withdrawn and must not be cited as evidence.
